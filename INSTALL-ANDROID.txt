ATLAS Estimating v2.9 — Android / TAPE Tool

This is the complete Android-only folder; do not mix iPad files.

1. Back up your existing ATLAS project(s) using Download Project Backup.
2. Upload the COMPLETE contents of this Android folder to your published Android repository/site.
3. In Android Chrome, open your site's android.html while online.
4. Reload once, then fully close/reopen the installed ATLAS app to refresh the offline cache.
5. Open a DXF, check units/calibrate, press 📏 TAPE, tap start and end.

TAPE is a temporary measuring overlay; it does not change takeoff quantities or saved projects.
Read README-TAPE-v2.9.md for controls and limitations.
